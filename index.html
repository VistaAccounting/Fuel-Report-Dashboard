Gallons by month — by driver and fuel type

Vista Field Equipment — B.Smart digital dispenses
Fuel dispensed through the B.Smart pump/kiosk at Vista Vineyards, August 2026. Includes bulk diesel tank refills (Z11-01/Z11-02) and foreman gas-can draws, later hand-distributed to tractors recorded on paper logs.
Most B.Smart equipment (forklifts, generators, ATVs, bulk tanks) carries no hours/mileage reading, so efficiency isn't available for those — ranked by total volume instead.
Gallons by month — by primary driver

Gallons by month — all equipment
Every EQ# across every transcribed paper log month on file, not just August — this table grows as more months get transcribed. Confirmed forklifts, tractors, and ATVs are matched against Equipment Inventory and Rental Equipment Summary automatically; anything needing judgment (shorthand, nicknames, no exact number) comes from asset_match_overrides.json. See Findings tab for flagged mismatches. JD Portal Hours is each unit's last known reading from the John Deere Portal (a snapshot as of the export date, not tied to any one month) — see the Engine Hours tab for make/model.
Fuel-type reconciliation — B.Smart pump vs. paper logs
Compares how much diesel was drawn from a bulk tank (Z11-01/Z11-02) against how much diesel was logged on paper for diesel-fueled field equipment — same idea for gasoline (any foreman's gas can vs. gasoline-fueled field equipment other than ATVs). ATVs get their own comparison: the ATV.NORTH/ATV.SOUTH gas cans against paper-logged ATV usage specifically, since those two gas cans only ever fuel the ATV fleet (Z15-01\u201305 = South, Z15-06\u201309 = North), not a mix of equipment like a foreman's own gas can. Not foreman-scoped: a bulk tank or gas can fills whatever equipment needs it, regardless of who did the filling or the distributing. Deliberately excludes fuel for a foreman's own forklift, truck, generator, or water truck — that fuel is used directly and never makes it onto a paper log. Limited to months where both B.Smart and paper log data are on file, since a month with only one side isn't a real comparison point.
